What the FDE does

Three stages, in order, then a person in the loop

Each stage stands on the one before it. There is no useful intelligence on data you cannot trust yet.

01 · Capture

Inventory every system you run, connect them, and reconcile on a schedule. The first visible output is small and honest: a daily list of the jobs where two systems disagree, and why.

  • ✓Inventory the systems — project management, ERP/accounting, payroll, field apps, and the spreadsheets on someone's desktop.
  • ✓Stand up connections — through their APIs where they exist, through an integration layer where that is cleaner than building a connector ourselves.
  • ✓Reconcile nightly — committed cost, invoiced-to-date and approved change orders matched across Procore and Sage into one table.
  • ✓Surface the disagreements — the specific jobs where the systems do not line up, named, with both values.
  • ✓Fix the data-entry gaps behind the disagreements, with your PMs and accounting team. The list gets shorter.

02 · Control

With the data trusted, the operational and financial picture — measured the same way on every job, updated daily instead of at month-end.

  • ✓Job costing and margin by job, phase and cost code — one method every job.
  • ✓Labour productivity against budgeted hours; where the field is losing time and on which activities.
  • ✓Subcontractor performance — on-time, rework, change-order volume, default-risk signals.
  • ✓Change-order and RFI cycle time — how long paper sits, and where.
  • ✓Cash — WIP, over/under billing, retainage held and owed, and a forward view.

03 · Intelligence

Ask in plain language and get answers computed against your data, with the underlying records attached.

  • ✓Plain-English questions answered against your data — “which jobs are fading margin faster than last month, and why?”
  • ✓Margin-leak alerts — jobs fading faster than trend, unbilled work aging, productivity dropping on a crew or activity.
  • ✓Cost-to-complete and cash forecasts that update as the source data changes.
  • ✓Suggested next steps a named person reviews and approves before anything happens.

Advisory, not autonomous

The AI drafts. It does not act. Every figure a client sees can be followed back to the record it came from.

  • ✓A model never originates a number. Displayed figures are computed from your records, with the line items attached.
  • ✓Nothing is sent, moved or changed — no email, no ERP update, no subcontractor notification — without a named person approving it.
  • ✓Where an LLM drafts a summary, it runs only against your scoped data, and its output is reviewed before anyone acts.

Yours to keep

The engagement is one person, priced against a hire — not a licence to a product you rent.

  • ✓The connections, data models, documentation and code the FDE builds live in your systems and stay yours.
  • ✓If the engagement ends you keep all of it, with a written handover.
  • ✓One monthly fee, reporting into your CFO or controller with a standing weekly review. See how the engagement works.
