The situation

Month-end is when you find out what a job made

A typical starting point: committed cost lives in Procore, actuals land in Sage on their own schedule, and the change orders that matter sit in a PM's inbox or a spreadsheet. The job-cost report is three weeks stale by the time anyone reads it, so the owner meeting runs on a number nobody fully trusts.

Capture

Make the data trustworthy first

The Forward Deployed Engineer inventories every system, stands up connections to Procore and Sage, and reconciles them on a nightly schedule. Committed cost, invoiced-to-date and approved change orders for every active job land in one table.

The first visible output is small and honest: a daily list of the three-or-so jobs where the two systems disagree, and why. That list gets shorter as the data-entry gaps behind it get fixed.

Control

Then make it legible

With the data trusted, the FDE builds the one-page job packet the PM takes into the owner meeting: current cost-to-complete, the change orders not yet in the owner's system, retainage exposure, and the two cost codes trending over budget — each figure linked to the invoices and timecards behind it.

There is no client quote to put here yet. When a contractor is willing to go on record, an approved sentence from their team will replace this note.

What you have at the end
  • One reconciled job-cost table
  • Nightly disagreement list
  • One-page job packet per job
  • Every figure traced to source
