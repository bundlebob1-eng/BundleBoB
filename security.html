Security & data

Home / Security & data
We will be inside your accounting and subcontractor data

Written to be checkable

No engagement has handled real client data yet, so most of what follows is design commitment rather than an audited, exercised control. Each card says which it is.

Tenancy and isolation

The commitment: each client firm's data stays separate from every other client's — in storage, in processing, and in anything an AI model sees. Nothing built or learned for one firm is reused in another's context, prompts or reports.

Design intent, not yet validated against a real client's data — there is no client yet. The intended model is per-firm storage under separate credentials, with every read, report and model prompt scoped to a single firm. The architecture is available for review under NDA.

Encryption

The commitment: client data encrypted in transit and at rest.

Design intent — current industry-standard TLS in transit, industry-standard ciphers at rest. The specific protocol versions, key management, and hosting provider and region are not yet fixed against a production client environment and will be documented before the first engagement.

Access — who can see what

The commitment: access limited to the Forward Deployed Engineer assigned to your firm and named colleagues who directly support them — least-privilege, and logged. We will not use your data for benchmarking, model training, marketing or any purpose other than your engagement.

Not yet exercised with a real client. The intent is that access is granted and revoked on your instruction and the access log is reviewable by you on request; that process exists on paper, not yet in practice.

AI and your data

By design, a model never originates a number — displayed figures are computed from your records, with the line items attached. Where an LLM drafts a summary it runs only against your scoped data, and a person reviews the output before any action.

Provider choices not yet finalised. Which model providers are used, and how prompt and output retention is handled, will be documented before the first engagement; we will agree in writing any data category that must never be sent to a model.

What is not in place yet

As of now there is no SOC 2 report, no third-party penetration test, and no signed BAA.

What is in place instead: written data-handling and access policies, the isolation and review design above, named accountability once an engagement begins, and a standing offer to complete your security questionnaire before you sign anything. This page is updated as that changes.

If an engagement ends

The commitment: you keep what was built, and your data is returned or destroyed on your instruction.

Not yet exercised — there has been no engagement to offboard. The intended process is export in a portable format (for example CSV or a database dump), deletion with written confirmation, and all connections, data models, documentation and code staying yours. Retention and deletion timelines will be set out in each engagement agreement.
