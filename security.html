Security & data

Home / Security & data
We will be inside your accounting and subcontractor data

Written to be checkable

Where a control is real, it is stated plainly. Where it is not in place yet, that is stated too, next to what is true instead.

Tenancy and isolation

Each client firm's data is kept separate from every other client's — in storage, in processing, and in anything an AI model sees. Nothing built or learned for one firm is reused in another's context, prompts or reports.

In practice: each firm's data is stored and queried under its own credentials, and every read, report and model prompt is scoped to a single firm. The full architecture is available for review under NDA.

Encryption

Client data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Transport uses current industry-standard TLS; data at rest uses industry-standard ciphers. The specific protocol versions, key management, and hosting provider and region are in our security documentation, shared on request.

Access — who can see what

Access is limited to the Forward Deployed Engineer assigned to your firm and named colleagues who directly support them. Least-privilege, and logged.

We do not use your data for benchmarking, model training, marketing or any purpose other than your engagement. Access is granted and revoked on your instruction; the access log is available to you on request.

AI and your data

A model never originates a number — displayed figures are computed from your records, with the line items attached. Where an LLM drafts a summary it runs only against your scoped data, and a person reviews the output before any action.

Which providers are used, and how prompt and output retention is handled, is documented and shared on request; we will agree in writing any data category that must never be sent to a model.

What is not in place yet

As of now there is no SOC 2 report, no third-party penetration test, and no signed BAA.

What is in place instead: written data-handling and access policies, the isolation and review model above, named accountability for every engagement, and a standing offer to complete your security questionnaire before you sign anything. This page is updated as that changes.

If an engagement ends

You keep what was built. Your data is returned or destroyed on your instruction.

Data is exported back to you in a portable format (CSV or database dump) and then deleted from our systems, with written confirmation. Connections, data models, documentation and code stay yours. Backup retention and deletion timelines are set out in the engagement agreement.
