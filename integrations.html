Systems we connect

Built around the systems you already run

The embedded engineer connects what your office and field have already standardised on — nobody is asked to migrate. This is the list we intend to support, ranked by what mid-market GCs run most. None of it is in production yet, because there is no client in production yet.

First pair

An ERP and a PM platform, reconciled

The pairing most engagements would start with: a construction ERP (Vista, Sage 300 CRE, Foundation, CMiC) and a project-management platform (Procore, Autodesk). Cost codes, commitments, change orders and field hours reconciled, with a conflict log a controller can actually read.
