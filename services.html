The engagement

One offer, not a
menu of deliverables

You are not buying modules or seats. You are adding one Forward Deployed Engineer — also called an AI Integrator — who works inside your firm, connects your systems, and builds the layer that shows where margin is leaking. Everything below is part of that one engagement, in roughly this order.

What the engineer actually does

The work, roughly in sequence

This is what the role is meant to cover. It is design intent for the engagement, not a description of one that has run — we are pre-pilot.

01

Cost-structure workshop

Time with your controller to map phases, cost types and codes as they actually exist — no reorganising your chart of accounts to fit a tool.

  • Mapping table you own
  • Payroll calendar captured
  • Conflict rules agreed up front
02

Data cleanup

Vendor crosswalks and duplicate resolution before the first sync, not after it causes a problem.

  • Tax-ID matching first
  • Proposed merges for review
  • Nothing merged without sign-off
03

Capture, then Control, then Intelligence

The arc, in order. Trustworthy unified data first; decision views second; plain-English answers last. No skipping ahead.

  • Every figure traces to a source record
  • A freshness stamp on every view
  • Advisory, not autonomous
04

Field adoption

The pilot runs with one foreman on their own phone, and the screen changes until they will use it.

  • Short crew pilot first
  • On site early
  • Changes shipped before wider rollout
05

Security-review support

The engineer completes your IT questionnaire and joins the call with your security team. What is implemented versus design intent is stated plainly.

  • Architecture overview
  • Data-flow diagram
  • Honest about gaps
06

Staying

The same named person who did the mapping stays on it — not a ticket queue that starts from zero each time. Anything reusable they build becomes part of the core.

  • One named contact
  • Regular review
  • Upgrade regression testing

How it is priced

Against a single mid-level hire, not per user and not per integration. It is a headcount line in your budget — the cost of the person you have been trying to hire for this — rather than a software subscription that grows with your project count. Exact figures are set with you, and are not published here yet.

What it is not

Not a consultancy that writes a report and leaves. Not an agency doing bespoke work that cannot generalise. Not an autonomous system making changes on its own — a person on your side approves every action, and every figure traces back to a source record you can open.
