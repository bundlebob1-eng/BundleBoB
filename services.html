One engagement, three stages, in order

Make the data trustworthy, then legible, then answerable

The Forward Deployed Engineer runs the same arc in every firm. Each stage stands on the one before it — there is no useful intelligence on data you cannot trust yet.

01 · Capture

Inventory every system — project management, ERP/accounting, payroll, field apps, and the spreadsheets on someone's desktop. Stand up connections, then reconcile on a schedule and surface where committed cost, actuals and change orders disagree.

See an example

02 · Control

Job costing by job, phase and cost code — same method every time. Labour productivity against budget. Subcontractor performance and default-risk signals. Change-order and RFI cycle time. Cash: WIP, over/under billing, retainage.

See an example

03 · Intelligence

Ask in plain language and get answers computed against your data, with the records attached. Margin-leak alerts, cost-to-complete and cash forecasts, and suggested actions a named person reviews and approves before anything happens.

See an example
Integration-heavy by design

We sit on top of what you already run

The question that matters is whether this works with your existing systems. We connect through their APIs where they exist, and through a third-party integration layer where that is cleaner than building a connector ourselves.

  • Procore, Sage 300 CRE, Foundation, Viewpoint, CMiC and other construction ERPs.
  • QuickBooks and Excel / Google Sheets — the spreadsheets nobody officially owns.
  • Field data from Autodesk / PlanGrid and photo / message tools.
