Overview

Trustworthy data first, then decisions

One Forward Deployed Engineer runs the same three stages in every firm. Capture makes the data trustworthy. Control makes it legible. Intelligence makes it answer questions — with a person approving every action and every figure traceable to a source record.

01 · Capture — what the FDE does

Inventory the systemsPM, ERP/accounting, payroll, field apps, and desktop spreadsheets.
Stand up connectionsThrough APIs, or an integration layer where that is cleaner.
Reconcile on a scheduleCommitted cost, actuals and change orders, matched nightly.
Surface disagreementsThe jobs where two systems do not agree, and why.

Example deliverable. By the end of the first month, committed cost and cost-to-date for every active job are pulled from Procore and Sage nightly into one table, with a short daily list of jobs where the two disagree.

02 · Control — the operational and financial picture

  • Job costing and margin by job, phase and cost code — same method every job.
  • Labour productivity against budgeted hours; where the field is losing time.
  • Subcontractor performance: on-time, rework, change-order volume, default-risk signals.
  • Change-order and RFI cycle time — how long paper sits, and where.
  • Cash: WIP and over/under billing, retainage held and owed, a forward view.

Example deliverable. Before the monthly owner meeting, the PM gets a one-page job packet: current cost-to-complete, the change orders not yet in the owner's system, retainage exposure, and the two cost codes trending over budget — each figure linked to its source line items.

03 · Intelligence — answers, with a person in the loop

  • Plain-language questions answered against your data, with records attached.
  • Margin-leak alerts: jobs fading faster than trend, unbilled work aging, productivity dropping.
  • Cost-to-complete and cash forecasts that update as the source data changes.
  • Suggested actions a named person reviews and approves before anything happens.

Example deliverable. Monday morning: “three jobs are fading margin faster than last month.” Each links to the change orders, labour overruns or vendor invoices driving it, and a suggested first step to accept or dismiss.
