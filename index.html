More about us

Who this is for

Mid-market general contractors already running Procore, Sage, Foundation or QuickBooks, with no one whose job is to make those systems agree — or to turn what comes out of them into a decision.

Who it is not for

Not startups. Not retail, SaaS or e-commerce. Not firms looking for a website, an app or a chatbot. If an offer would make sense outside commercial construction, it is not this one.

How the work runs

Each stage stands on the one before it

The output at every step is something a person on your team reads and acts on — never a black box.

01

Inventory

Map every system you run — project management, ERP, payroll, field apps, and the spreadsheets on someone's desktop.

02

Connect

Stand up connections into those systems — through their APIs, or a third-party integration layer where that is cleaner.

03

Reconcile

Match committed cost, actuals and change orders across systems, and surface the jobs where they disagree.

04

Answer

Plain-English questions answered against your data, with the underlying line items attached and a person in the loop.
