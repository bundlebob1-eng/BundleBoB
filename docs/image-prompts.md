# Generated business imagery

Method: built-in `image_gen` tool, using the `imagegen` skill. New images, without reference-photo inputs. Selected outputs were copied into this project, converted to JPEG source assets, and encoded as WebP at quality 78 with 768px variants. These scenes illustrate business contexts; they are not customer photographs. The website footer states that distinction.

## Workshop hero

Files: `assets/images/workshop.jpg`, `assets/images/workshop.webp`, `assets/images/workshop-768.webp`, and the 564×941 responsive crop `assets/images/workshop-mobile.webp`.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero photograph for BundleBoB, an operations and job profitability software company. Create an editorial photograph, landscape 16:9, at least 1536 pixels wide. A real-feeling independent auto and equipment workshop in late afternoon, an experienced female shop owner and a male technician in navy work shirts reviewing a tablet beside a workbench on the RIGHT HALF of the composition. Natural gestures, calm confidence, candid interaction, they are focused on the work and not looking at the camera. Workshop and partially visible vehicle in background, subtle warm natural light through tall industrial windows. LEFT HALF is softly out-of-focus darker workshop space with plenty of clean negative space for white headline overlay, no important subjects on left. Premium cinematic commercial photography with realistic skin and materials, rich warm neutrals, subdued blues, gentle grain and shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no graphics, no website UI. Keep the tablet screen turned away and unreadable. Horizontal environmental composition, not a headshot.
